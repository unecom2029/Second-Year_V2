🎯 Mood Disorders — High-Yield One-Pager

Condensed by learning objective — LO1 Symptoms & Timeline · LO2 Treatment Options · LO3 Differentiation by Indication/Side Effects/Choice

LO 1 · SYMPTOMS & TIMELINE

Episode Durations

  • Manic: ≥7d, OR hospitalization/psychosis — AND impairment
  • Hypomanic: ≥4d, NO psychosis/marked impairment
  • MDE: ≥2wk, 5+/9 SIG E CAPS (mood or anhedonia required)
  • PDD: ≥2yr (1yr peds), 3+ milder symptoms
  • Cyclothymic: ≥2yr hypomania-lite + depression-lite, never full episode

Mnemonics

  • DIGFAST (mania): Distractibility, Impulsivity, Grandiosity, Flight of ideas, Activity↑, Sleep need↓, Talkativeness
  • SIG E CAPS (depression): Sleep, Interest↓, Guilt, Energy↓, Concentration↓, Appetite, Psychomotor, Suicidality
  • SAD PERSONS: Sex(M), Age, Depression, Prior attempt (#1 risk factor), EtOH/drug, Rational thought loss, Sickness, Organized plan, No support, Stated intent

Disorders at a Glance

  • MDD = 1+ MDE
  • PDD = chronic mild depression ≥2yr
  • Bipolar I = 1+ manic episode (depression not required)
  • Bipolar II = hypomania + MDE, never a manic episode
  • Cyclothymic = hypomania-lite + depression-lite ≥2yr

Bipolar vs. Borderline PD

  • Bipolar: episodes last weeks–months, recur ~every couple years, ↓sleep need is core
  • BPD: swings hours–days, multiple times per day/week, sleep not disrupted the same way
LO 2 · TREATMENT OPTIONS

Depression Treatment Ladder

  • Mild: lifestyle changes ± app-based therapy
  • Moderate: therapy or antidepressant
  • Severe: therapy and antidepressant
  • Resistant: augment (2 antidepressants / +SGA / +mood stabilizer / +thyroid) → ECT → ketamine/esketamine, TMS

Antidepressant Line of Therapy

  • 1st: SSRI = SNRI (tied)
  • 2nd: TCA
  • 3rd: "Others" — bupropion, mirtazapine, trazodone
  • 4th: MAOI

Gold Standards & Key Facts

  • Gold standard for depression = therapy + medication
  • Gold standard for mania = lithium
  • 4 treatments proven to ↓ suicide: ECT, esketamine, lithium, clozapine
  • Recurrence risk climbs every episode → duration: 1st ep. ≥1yr, 2nd ep. 2–3yr, 3rd ep. lifetime

Bipolar Treatment by Phase

  • Mania: lithium, valproate, carbamazepine, 2nd-gen antipsychotic — all 1st-line
  • Depression: lamotrigine 1st-line; lithium/carbamazepine also help; valproate does not
  • Maintenance: lithium (best evidence), carbamazepine, lamotrigine
  • Fluoxetine+olanzapine = only FDA-approved antidepressant combo, maintenance only (not acute mania)
  • 2nd-gen antipsychotics used: aripiprazole, olanzapine, quetiapine, risperidone, ziprasidone — haloperidol is 1st-gen despite appearing in med tables; don't pick it if a question specifically asks for "2nd-generation"

Interventional / Procedural Options

  • ECT: gold standard, safe in pregnancy/elderly, no absolute contraindications
  • Ketamine (IV) / Esketamine (intranasal): treatment-resistant depression w/o psychotic features
  • TMS: non-invasive, no anesthesia, ~5x/week — lower yield than ECT/ketamine
LO 3 · DIFFERENTIATION

Antidepressants — Named Drugs

  • SSRI: Sertraline (most Rx'd, well tolerated) · Fluoxetine (longest t½, 2D6 inhibitor, also FDA for bulimia) · Escitalopram (no QTc effect) · Citalopram (QTc risk — older) · Fluvoxamine (OCD only) · Paroxetine (shortest t½, "dirty" — many interactions)
  • SNRI: Venlafaxine (dose-dependent, low=SSRI-like/high=NE, ↑BP) · Desvenlafaxine (NE-specific metabolite) · Duloxetine (+ neuropathic pain/fibromyalgia)
  • TCA — tertiary (more SE): Amitriptyline (pain) · Imipramine (pain/IBS/enuresis) · Clomipramine (OCD/panic) · Doxepin (bipolar depression/insomnia)
  • TCA — secondary (fewer SE): Nortriptyline (cyclic vomiting/IBS/pain/migraine/smoking cessation) · Desipramine (ADHD)
  • MAOI: Tranylcypromine · Phenelzine · Isocarboxazid · Selegiline (patch, MAO-B selective, less strict diet)
  • Others: Bupropion — NDRI, smoking cessation, ↓seizure threshold (black box) · Mirtazapine — alpha-2 antagonist, wt gain/sedation · Trazodone — mainly insomnia, priapism · Vilazodone/Vortioxetine — newer, low yield beyond MOA

Serotonin Syndrome vs. NMS

  • 5-HT syndrome: hours, hyperreflexia/clonus, autonomic instability, cross-mechanism combos = highest risk
  • NMS: days, "lead-pipe" rigidity, hyporeflexia, high fever, follows antipsychotics

Quick Pick by Scenario

  • Depression + pain → SNRI or TCA
  • Depression + smoker → Bupropion
  • Depression + insomnia/↓appetite → Mirtazapine
  • OCD → Fluvoxamine (SSRI) or Clomipramine (TCA)
  • Acute mania, hospitalized → Lithium
  • Bipolar depression → Lamotrigine
