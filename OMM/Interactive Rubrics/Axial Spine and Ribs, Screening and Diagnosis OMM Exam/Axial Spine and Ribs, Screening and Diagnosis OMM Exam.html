Axial Spine and Ribs — Screening and Diagnosis
1COMMUNICATION: Appropriately communicates to patient about the physical exam being done (uses lay terms, speaks to pt, assures understanding).
2Obtains consent before doing the exam AND again with any particularly sensitive parts of the exam.
3WASHES HANDS: Prior to technique (may do at beginning of encounter) – hand sanitizer, or at least 20s with soap and water (if student is cued to do this task, then no credit will be given).
4HEAD/CERVICAL SPINE SCREENING: Effectively segmentally screens the entire cervical spine (OA-C7) with appropriate contact and force vector bilaterally.
5HEAD/CERVICAL SPINE SEGMENTAL DIAGNOSIS: Correctly performs a 3-positional segmental diagnosis of the OA, AA, and most restricted lower cervical (C2-C7) segment AND is accurate with the diagnosis.
6THORACIC SPINE SCREENING: Effectively segmentally screens the entire thoracic spine (T1-T12) AND the thoracic outlet with appropriate contact and force vector bilaterally.
7THORACIC SPINE SEGMENTAL DIAGNOSIS: Correctly performs a 3-positional segmental diagnosis of the most restricted thoracic (T1-T12) segment AND the thoracic outlet AND is accurate with the diagnosis.
8RIB CAGE SCREENING: Effectively screens the entire rib cage (ribs 1-12) for respiratory (inhaled/exhaled) AND mechanical (anterior/posterior) dysfunctions with appropriate contact and force vector bilaterally.
9RIB CAGE DIAGNOSIS: Correctly performs a positional segmental diagnosis of the most restricted ribs AND is accurate with the diagnosis.
