🎯 Application Session 1 — Chest Pain & Secondary HTN

Watkins (CAD case module) · Segal (HTN case) · OMK 2 Cardio · October 2026

CHEST PAIN → SIHD

History

  • Quality, location, onset, duration, relief, associated sx + "can you make it occur?"
  • Ischemic: pressure, substernal → neck/jaw, minutes, exertion/stress, predictable
  • Non-ischemic: sharp/pleuritic/positional, ripping to back, seconds, pinpoint; NTG relief not diagnostic
  • Women: smoking 6× (men 3×), DM more potent, CAD ~10 y later

Exam & work-up

  • Pulses, bruits, S3, edema, xanthomas · FH: tendon xanthoma, arcus < 45 (xanthelasma low spec)
  • CBC, CMP, A1c, lipids, Lp(a) once · ECG for all · TTE · no outpatient troponin
  • CAD Consortium pre-test prob 40–50% = intermediate → testing
  • Can exercise + interpretable ECG: low-int → ETT; int-high → stress echo/MPI or CCTA · can't exercise → pharmacologic stress or CCTA
  • Stress echo > ETT, esp. in women

Antianginals

  • BB 1st line (↓ HR, contractility) · nitrates 2nd (↓ preload; CI HOCM, PDE-5i)
  • DHP CCB ↓ afterload · non-DHP ↓ HR/inotropy (CI severe AS, HOCM, low EF)
  • Ivabradine = If (HCN) · ranolazine = late Na⁺ (↑ QTc)
  • OMT = antiplatelet + statin + ACEi/ARB + BB
ACS · MI TYPES · COMPLICATIONS

Crescendo → NSTEMI

  • No stress test with active ischemia · never by car · office: aspirin, NTG, O₂ prn, monitor → ambulance
  • ER: ACS protocol, serial ECG/hs-cTn, POCUS, IV NTG, heparin, clopidogrel load
  • Invasive: very high (shock, refractory pain, VT/arrest, mechanical, AHF) < 2 h · high (NSTEMI, dynamic ST, GRACE > 140) < 24 h · intermediate < 72 h
  • STEMI door-to-balloon < 90 min

MI classification

  • Primary (type 1) = plaque/thrombus; younger men · Secondary (type 2) = supply–demand; older women, comorbid · Procedural
  • CFR: max flow ↓ from ~50% stenosis; rest flow until 80–90%
  • hs-cTn = gold standard (up 7–10 days)

Mechanical complications

  • Papillary: inferior MI, posteromedial (PDA), ≤ 7 d, pulm edema, apical murmur → axilla
  • VSR: 3–5 d, LSB holosystolic + thrill, O₂ step-up → ICU, surgery
  • Free wall: anterior, > 70, women, 24 h or 3–5 d, tamponade, PEA
  • Dx: ECG + echo
HTN → PRIMARY ALDOSTERONISM

Measure & classify

  • Seated 5 min, back/feet supported, arm at heart, both arms, right cuff, bare arm; avg ≥ 2 readings on ≥ 2 visits
  • Cuff over clothing ±5–50 mm Hg
  • Elevated 120–129/< 80 · St 1 130–139 or 80–89 · St 2 ≥ 140 or ≥ 90 · > 180/120 severe → check TOD
  • White-coat vs masked (ABPM/HBPM)

Suspect secondary

  • Resistant, abrupt, < 30 y, loss of control, disproportionate TOD, malignant, new DBP ≥ 65 y, unprovoked ↓K⁺
  • Resistant HTN: OSA > 30%, PA 6–23%
  • ↓R ↑A = PA · ↑R ↑A = RAS/diuretics · ↓R ↓A = Liddle, licorice, Cushing, CAH

Primary aldosteronism

  • Most common curable endocrine HTN (5–14%); K⁺ usually normal
  • Screen: ES 2025 everyone; ACC/AHA 2025 resistant HTN
  • ARR: AM, seated; correct K⁺; hold MRA 4 wk; BB false +, ACEi/ARB/diuretic false −; only meaningful if renin suppressed
  • 60% bilateral, 30% adenoma · CT + AVS → unilateral adrenalectomy / bilateral spironolactone (eplerenone), amiloride
  • No edema = aldosterone escape (pressure natriuresis + ANP) · ↓K⁺ → nephrogenic DI polyuria
  • PA vs essential: AF OR 3.5, stroke, LVH, HF, CAD
