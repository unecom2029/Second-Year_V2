🎯 Application Session 2 — Heart Failure & PAD

Segal · Scully · Qazi · OMK 2 Cardio · October 2026

HFpEF CASE

Work-up

  • Dyspnea buckets: cardiac, pulm, anemia, neuromuscular, anxiety (last)
  • Edema: ↑ hydrostatic (HF) vs ↓ oncotic (cirrhosis, nephrotic)
  • S4 = stiff LV · S3 = volume overload (or young) · JVP = height + 5
  • Labs: CBC, CMP, TSH, A1C, iron, NP · no office troponin
  • BNP active, t½ 20 min, rule-out ≤ 100, ↑ by ARNI · NT-proBNP inert, 1–2 h, ≤ 300, age cut-offs 450/900/1800
  • LVH "11 and 35" (age ≥ 40) · concentric = pressure, eccentric = volume
  • Diastolic grade 1 = normal LA pressure (normal for age) · grade 2 = ↑ LA pressure
  • H₂FPEF: BMI>30 2, ≥2 BP meds 1, AF 3, PASP>35 1, age>60 1, E/e′>9 1

Classify

  • HFrEF ≤ 40 · HFmrEF 41–49 · HFpEF ≥ 50 · HFimpEF ≤40→>40
  • Right HF: ↑JVP, edema, hepatomegaly, ascites · Left: orthopnea, PND, crackles · #1 cause of right HF = left HF
  • High output: thyrotoxic, AV fistula, obesity, beriberi (↑CO ↓SVR)
  • HFpEF: older women, HTN/DM/obesity/CKD/AF; HFrEF: younger men post-MI

HFpEF treatment

  • DASH/Mediterranean, exercise (−16% mortality), no routine fluid restriction, weight, OSA
  • Everyone: loop ± thiazide; SGLT2i unless CI · Some: GLP-1 (BMI ≥ 30), MRA (K ≤ 4.7, eGFR ≥ 30) · ACEi/ARB/ARNI not 1°
DIURETICS · ADHF · GDMT

Diuretic pharm

  • Loop (NKCC2, TAL 20–30% of Na⁺) first · braking = DCT hypertrophy · Lasix "lasts six" then 18 h retention
  • Add thiazide (NCC) = sequential blockade
  • Both → ENaC → hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis; loop also ↓Mg, ↓Ca
  • SGLT2i: A1C ↓ 0.5% only; natriuretic; TGF → afferent constriction
  • Spironolactone gynecomastia → eplerenone (BID); finerenone non-steroidal

Decompensated HFrEF

  • EtOH (± thiamine/wet beriberi), cocaine, iron overload, IE, myocarditis, ischemia
  • Cocaine + β₁-selective BB → unopposed α → ↑ afterload; carvedilol/labetalol · cocaethylene · check CK
  • Flat mild troponin = secondary MI · Kerley B lines
  • Forrester: wet–warm → floor, IV diuretics · wet–cold → ICU, inotropes · dry–cold → fluids, ± inotropes
  • Preload first (diurese), then afterload · no new BB while decompensated

Four pillars

  • ARNI > ACEi >> ARB · BB (metoprolol succ, carvedilol, bisoprolol) · MRA · SGLT2i
  • RRR mortality: BB 35%, MRA 30%, ARNI switch 28%, ACEi/SGLT2i 17%
  • HR 0.84 → 0.36 with all four · ~5 y longer (RRR 73%, NNT 3.9)
  • Start all low, in hospital; 7–10 d follow-up; watch K⁺/Cr
  • Ivabradine (If) · digoxin · vericiguat = sGC stimulator
PAD

Recognize

  • Vascular: cramping, reproducible, relieved by standing still in minutes, ↓ pulses/ABI · Neurogenic: relieved by flexion, shopping cart, uphill
  • Smoking 2–4× (persists 30 y), DM → severe, HTN 40%, M = F, age
  • Nocebo: statin "side effects" mostly expectation (SAMSON)
  • CLTI ≥ 2 wk rest pain/ulcer/gangrene, dependent rubor · ALI < 2 wk, 6 Ps → IV heparin, emergent surgery
  • Buttock/hip = aortoiliac · upper calf = SFA · lower calf = popliteal · Leriche: claudication + ↓ femorals + ED

Test

  • Arm BP difference < 10 · PP ≈ SV/compliance
  • ABI = highest ankle / highest arm · ≤ 0.90 PAD · 0.91–0.99 borderline · > 1.40 non-compressible → TBI · < 0.40 critical
  • Doppler triphasic → biphasic → monophasic

Treat

  • SET first (30–45 min, ≥ 3/wk, 12 wk) · pentoxifylline no role
  • High-intensity statin, BP, DM, smoking cessation, foot care
  • SAPT (clopidogrel or aspirin) · aspirin + rivaroxaban 2.5 BID ↓ MACE + MALE
  • Cilostazol (PDE3) helps walking; CI in any HF · vorapaxar = PAR-1, ↑ ICH
  • Varenicline/cytisine (α4β2 partial agonist), bupropion, NRT, quitline
  • 5-y: MI/stroke 20%, death 15–30% » CLI 1–2% · polyvascular 1-y events 5 → 26%
