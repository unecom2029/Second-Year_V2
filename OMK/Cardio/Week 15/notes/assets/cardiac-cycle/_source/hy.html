THE NORMAL CYCLE

Valve rule

  • Flow goes high → low pressure. A valve opens when the proximal chamber is higher, closes when the distal one overtakes
  • LVP > LAP: mitral closes (S1) · LVP > AoP: aortic opens · LVP < AoP: aortic closes (S2, dicrotic notch) · LVP < LAP: mitral opens
  • P before atrial contraction · QRS before ventricular contraction · T = repolarization in late ejection

Seven phases

  • 1 atrial systole (a wave, kick, S4) · 2 isovolumetric contraction (all closed, P ↑, V flat, c wave)
  • 3 rapid / 4 reduced ejection (aortic open, T wave, v builds)
  • 5 isovolumetric relaxation (all closed, P ↓, V flat, v peaks)
  • 6 rapid filling (most filling, S3) · 7 diastasis (shortens first with ↑ HR)

Waves & notch

  • a = atrial contraction · c = AV valve bulges back at onset of systole · v = atrial filling
  • AF: no a wave, no kick, no S4
  • Dicrotic notch = aortic closure + elastic recoil
  • Normal: LV and aortic peaks superimposed; LA and LV diastolic pressures superimposed

Numbers

  • Ao 120/80 · LV 120/10 · LA ~10 · EDV ~130, ESV ~50 mL
  • SV = EDV − ESV · EF = SV/EDV (50–70%) · CO = SV × HR (~5 L/min)
  • Atrial kick ≈ 20–25% of output (lecture)
SOUNDS & OUTPUT

Heart sounds

  • S1 AV closure · S2 semilunar closure
  • S3 right after S2: rapid filling of a volume-overloaded LV (HFrEF, MR, AR); can be normal in the young · "Ken-tuck-y"
  • S4 just before S1: kick into a stiff LV (LVH, AS, hypertension) · "Ten-nes-see" · recording said "before S2" (slip)
  • Paradoxical split S2: severe AS, LBBB · fixed: ASD

Heart rate

  • Healthy: ↑ HR + ↑ contractility + stretch → ↑ CO
  • Sick heart: tachycardia cuts filling time → ↓ SV → ↓ CO

Two-question method

  • Systolic murmur → AS or MR · diastolic → MS or AR
  • Stenosis → gradient · regurgitation → extra volume
  • Pressure overload → concentric LVH · volume overload → dilation (eccentric)

Mitral apparatus

  • 2 leaflets of ~equal area · chordae from both papillary muscles go to both leaflets · the LV is part of the apparatus

AS

  • Bimodal: bicuspid (40s–60s; screen for ascending aneurysm + coarctation) vs calcific (70s+; lamellar bone; statins don't help)
  • Exercise intolerance → angina → exertional syncope → HF · LA always high (stiff LV)
  • Severe: late-peaking murmur, soft S2 · ≥ 4 m/s, ≥ 40 mm Hg, ≤ 1.0 cm² · TAVR first-line · avoid nitrates

MS · MR · AR extras

  • MS: severe ≤ 1.5 cm²; short A2–OS = severe; AF + LA clot (warfarin); PBMC
  • MR: MVP = myxomatous, chordal rupture → flail → acute HF · secondary: dilated LV, inferior MI (posteromedial papillary) · repair over replace
  • AR: endocarditis, bicuspid, Marfan / root, dissection (acute) · Corrigan, de Musset, Quincke, Austin Flint
