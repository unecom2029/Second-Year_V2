Control of the circulation

  • α1 Gq vasoconstriction · α2 Gi brake · β1 Gs heart + renin · β2 Gs dilation · M2 Gi slows SA/AV · NN in all ganglia. Vessels: no parasympathetic supply.
  • Odd = Gq, even = Gi, betas are Gs. Smooth muscle: MLCK contracts; PKA (cAMP) and PKG (cGMP, NO) relax.
  • Pacemaker: phase 4 funny Na⁺ (slope = rate) · phase 0 Ca²⁺ · phase 3 K⁺. Myocyte: phase 0 Na⁺ · plateau Ca²⁺ vs K⁺ · phase 3 K⁺.
  • AV delay ~0.1 s · Purkinje fastest, AV node slowest · denervated HR ~100 · relative refractory period = arrhythmia window.
  • MAP = CO × TPR (MAP is the dependent variable). ACE = kininase II (bradykinin → cough).
  • Poiseuille: R ∝ 1/r⁴ (½ radius → 1/16 flow) · capillaries slowest · parallel lowers total R.
  • ~70% of blood in veins · Pmsf ~7–8 set by stressed volume · nitrate + sildenafil collapses it · Guyton crossing = steady state.
  • HF paradox: pressure systems beat the volume reflex → retention, dilutional hyponatremia (low urine Na⁺).
  • Phenylephrine → reflex bradycardia (atropine) · M2 block + NE → HR ↑ BP ↑ · dobutamine + PDE3i both ↑cAMP.
