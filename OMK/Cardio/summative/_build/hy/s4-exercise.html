Exercise, rehab & prevention

  • 75–90% of CAD from diet, inactivity, smoking. Exercise: ↑vagal/↓sympathetic, ↑HDL, ↑NO, ↓CRP, ↑ischemic threshold.
  • HRmax = 220 − age · 1 MET ≈ 3.5 mL O₂/kg/min · SV plateaus at ~50% VO₂max, then HR drives CO.
  • SBP ↑ 8–12 mm Hg per MET · DBP flat or ↓ (TPR falls).
  • Each 1 MET → 8–35% ↓ mortality (RR 0.87/MET). ≥150 min moderate or ≥75 min vigorous a week (class I).
  • Rehab phase II = 36 sessions, ~3/wk, ECG-monitored, 8–12 wk. Start 1–2 wk (PCI/MI) · 2–6 wk (CABG).
  • Class I: ACS <12 mo, CABG/PCI, stable angina, PAD · class IIa: chronic HF. Only 30–40% enrol; physician endorsement is the lever.
  • FITT: ≥3×/wk · 40–80% HR reserve, RPE 11–16 · 30–60 min · angina → ~10 beats below the ischemic threshold.
  • Mediterranean (PREDIMED, HR 0.70) = only diet with RCT event reduction · DASH + low sodium add · Na <2,400 mg (<1,500 better) · trans fats = class III harm.
