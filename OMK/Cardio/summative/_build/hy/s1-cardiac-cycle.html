Cardiac cycle & PV loops

  • Blood flows high → low pressure; a valve opens when the chamber before it is higher. LV > LA: mitral shuts (S1) · LV > aorta (~80): aortic opens · LV < aorta: aortic shuts (S2, dicrotic notch) · LV < LA: mitral opens.
  • Isovolumetric contraction and relaxation: all four valves closed, volume flat.
  • a = atrial contraction · c = AV valve bulging at the onset of systole · v = atrial filling. AF: no a wave, no kick, no S4.
  • Kick: Qazi 20–25% of output, Morganelli ~10% of EDV. Diastasis shortens first as HR rises.
  • Ao 120/80 · LV 120/8–10 · LA 8–10 · PA 25/10 · RV 25–30/4 · RA 0–4. SV ≈ 70 · EF 55–70% · CO 4–5 L/min · MAP 90–95.
  • Ca²⁺ binds troponin C, never tropomyosin. Cardiac = calcium-induced calcium release (L-type → RyR2). Heart works on the ascending limb → Starling.
  • Boards use PV loops, not Wiggers. Width = SV · ESPVR slope = contractility · EDPVR = stiffness.
  • ↑Afterload: taller + narrower (ESV ↑, SV ↓) · ↑preload: wider right · ↑contractility: steeper ESPVR, ESV ↓.
  • Acute MI, first seconds: lower Starling curve at a higher EDV.
