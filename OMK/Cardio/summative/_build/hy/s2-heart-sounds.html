Heart sounds

  • S1 = mitral + tricuspid close · S2 = aortic + pulmonic close. Normal valve opening is silent.
  • S3 right after S2 = volume overload (HFrEF, dilated cardiomyopathy, MR, AR); normal in the young · "Ken-tuck-y".
  • S4 just before S1 = kick into a stiff LV (hypertension, AS, LVH) · gone in AF · "Ten-nes-see". (Recording said "before S2" — slip.)
  • Split S2: physiologic wider on inspiration · wide = PS, RBBB · fixed = ASD · paradoxical = severe AS, LBBB.
  • Opening snap = MS (short S2–snap = severe) · midsystolic click = MVP (earlier on standing) · soft S2 = severe AS.
