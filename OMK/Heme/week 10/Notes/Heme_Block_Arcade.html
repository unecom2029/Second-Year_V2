How to use this file

The Heme Block Arcade

Eleven games and a boss round, drawing on all three of the block's lectures at once. The notes files teach; this one tests. Nothing here has a section heading giving the answer away, and the items are deliberately shuffled across lectures — because the exam will not tell you which lecture a question came from either.

🔑 How to use it
  • Each game reshuffles when you exhaust its item bank, so it does not become a memorization sequence.
  • The score is per game and resets on reload — it is a practice signal, not a grade.
  • Work through a game until you are getting them right for the right reason. The feedback text explains the mechanism every time, including when you were correct.
  • Start with Boss Round if you want to find your weak spots first, then go back to the specific game.

🪝 Memory hook — If you only carry five numbers into the exam
  • 80–100 fL — the MCV split. Everything in the anemia differential hangs off it.
  • 7 / 10,000 / 30% — red cells, platelets, plasma. The three transfusion thresholds.
  • Retic LOW = production problem; retic HIGH = destruction or loss. One test halves the tree.
  • Ferritin LOW + TIBC HIGH = iron deficiency. Ferritin HIGH + TIBC LOW = inflammation.
  • MMA elevated = B12. MMA normal = folate. Homocysteine rises in both and settles nothing.

Anemia I — production

Framework

  • Blood loss · impaired production · increased destruction.
  • MCV <80 micro · 80–100 normo · >100 macro.
  • Retic LOW = production. Retic HIGH = loss or destruction.
  • Hct ≈ 3 × Hgb. Normal Hct 37–47% F, 42–52% M.

Iron deficiency

  • Fe ↓, ferritin ↓, TIBC ↑, saturation ↓ (<15%), MCV ↓, marrow iron absent.
  • Causes: dietary lack · malabsorption · ↑ requirement · chronic blood loss.
  • Adult male or postmenopausal female = GI blood loss until proven otherwise.
  • Ferritin is an acute phase reactant — falsely normal when inflamed.

B12 / folate / inflammation

  • B12 = DNA synthesis + myelin. Folate = DNA only, no neurology.
  • MMA elevated = B12. MMA normal = folate. Homocysteine rises in both.
  • Macro-ovalocytes + hypersegmented neutrophils; high LDH with low retics = ineffective erythropoiesis.
  • Pernicious anemia: anti-parietal cell 85–90%, anti-IF 75%; gastric atrophy → cancer risk; subacute combined degeneration.
  • Never give folate alone to a macrocytic anemia.
  • AOCD: hepcidin ↑ locks ferroportin. Ferritin ↑, TIBC ↓. Treat the disease.
Anemia II — hemolysis

Intravascular vs extravascular

  • All hemolysis: short lifespan · bilirubin ↑ · retics ↑.
  • Intravascular: schistocytes, pink plasma, hemoglobinuria, haptoglobin ABSENT, kidney at risk.
  • Extravascular: spherocytes, haptoglobin mildly ↓, splenomegaly.

The four defects

  • HS — autosomal dominant, cytoskeleton (ANK1/SPTB/SLC4A1). Spherocytes, osmotic fragility ↑, Coombs NEGATIVE. Splenectomy.
  • G6PD — X-linked, enzyme unstable so older cells deficient. Bite/blister cells, Heinz bodies. Triggers: infection, antimalarials, sulfa, nitrofurantoin, fava beans. Assay falsely normal in crisis.
  • Thalassemia — 4 alpha (chr 16), 2 beta (chr 11). Damage is from the excess unpaired chain. Target cells, MCV ↓, iron studies normal — do NOT give iron. HbH = β₄; Hb Barts = γ₄.
  • SCD — valine for glutamic acid, HBB. Trait 40–45% HbS + HbA; disease 90–100% HbS, no HbA. Crises: vaso-occlusive · aplastic (retics LOW) · sequestration (spleen huge). Hydroxyurea ↑ HbF; penicillin + vaccines; keep HbS <30%.
Transfusion

Products & thresholds

  • RBC 1–6 °C / 42 d · platelets room temp agitated / 5–7 d · plasma & cryo frozen / 1 yr.
  • TRICC: restrictive 7 g/dL; liberal arm did worse.
  • Platelets 10,000 hypoproliferative · 20,000 lumbar puncture · 25,000 preterm neonate · dengue: none.
  • 30% factor activity is enough to clot — above it plasma is a teacup into an ocean.
  • Cryo = fibrinogen. Warfarin → PCC + vitamin K.

Immunohematology

  • A → anti-B · B → anti-A · AB → neither · O → both, from gut colonization.
  • ABO = IgM = complement = INTRAVASCULAR. Alloantibody = IgG = EXTRAVASCULAR.
  • O = universal RBC donor; AB = universal RBC recipient. Inverts for plasma.
  • 15% RhD negative; O neg ≈ 6%. Save O negative for females of childbearing potential.
  • Screen = ready. Cross = reserved. Direct Coombs = patient’s cells; indirect = patient’s plasma.

Reactions

  • STOP · IV open · clerical check · treat · send to blood bank.
  • Febrile non-hemolytic — cytokines; ↓ by leukoreduction. Commonest.
  • Septic — platelets; skin plug. Most common infectious risk.
  • Acute hemolytic — pink plasma, hemoglobinuria, positive DAT. Never event, from wrong blood in tube.
  • Anaphylaxis — tiny volume; consider IgA deficiency.
  • TRALI — hypotension, normal JVP, donor anti-HLA. TACO — hypertension, raised JVP, diuresis works.
  • GVHD — pancytopenia weeks later, fatal. Prevented only by IRRADIATION.
