M NI U
Introduced self, asked the patient's name, preferred way to be addressed, DOB, and Age
Sanitized hands with air dry or washed hands at sink for 20 seconds, before AND after touching the patient and when appropriate
Obtained consent for the patient exam, acknowledged the patient's ability to relinquish consent at any time
Supported the patient with comfort and empathy measures during exam
Spoke only to the patient during the exam, explained what was necessary, and used non-medical jargon to explain things
Was respectful and professional in manner and demeanor throughout the exam to student patient and faculty facilitator proctor

Musculoskeletal System Exams for the Shoulder
*P0 Test / Maneuver
OSE Static — Anterior View: Facial Symmetry, Head/Neck Head Carriage, Shoulder Angle Hts, Thoracic Cage Position, Pelvis-Iliac Crest Hts, Hip-Greater Trochanter Hts, Knee-Tibia/Femur Angle or Condyle Hts, Ankle-Medial Arch or Malleoli Hts BL 1
OSE Static — Lateral View: Cervical, Thoracic, and Lumbar Curves, Head-External Auditory Meatus, Humerus-Greater Tuberosity, Femur-Greater Trochanter, Femur-Lateral Condyle, Ankle-Lateral Malleolus Alignments BL 2
OSE Static — Posterior View: Head-Mastoid Process Hts, Shoulder Angle Hts, Inferior Scapular Angle Hts, Spine Curves, Pelvis-Iliac Crest Hts, Hip-Greater Trochanter Hts, Knee-Tibia/Femur Angle or Condyle Hts, Ankle-Lateral Malleoli Hts BL 3
Inspect Cervical, Thoracic, Shoulder, and Elbow (usually affected joint, joint above, joint below, and adjacent joints): Inspect the skin for abrasions, bruising, scars, muscle for bulk and tone, and symmetry of the affected region BL 4
Palpate Cervical, Thoracic, Shoulder, and Elbow: Palpate for TART BL 5
AROM of the Cervical Spine: Flex, Ext, Sidebend R, Sidebend L, Rotate R, Rotate L 6
AROM of the Thoracic Spine: Flex, Ext, Sidebend R, Sidebend L, Rotate R, Rotate L 7
AROM of the Elbow: Flex, Ext, Pronate, Supinate, but note carrying angle BL 8
AROM of the Shoulder: Flex, Ext, AB, AD, IR, ER BL 9
PROM of the Shoulder: Flex, Ext, AB, AD, IR, ER BL 10
Test Strength of the Shoulder: Flex, Ext, AB, AD, IR, ER vs. resistance BL 11
Partial Peripheral Nervous System Exams (please ask permission if removing garments in sensitive areas)
Test Any UE Myotomes not Tested Previously with Strength Testing: C5 shoulder abduction, C6 elbow flexion OR wrist extension, C7 elbow extension OR wrist flexion, C8 grip OR finger flexion AND T1 finger abduction AND adduction OR C8-T1 finger and thumb opposition BL 12
UE Dermatomes: Use Light Touch to test C5 lateral upper arm, C6 dorsum of thumb, C7 dorsum of middle finger, C8 dorsum of little finger, T1 medial forearm BL (ASIA sensory points are underlined) 13
UE Deep Tendon Reflexes: C5 Biceps, C6 Brachioradialis AND C7 Triceps reflexes BL 14
OSE TART and Boney Regional Testing (which might have been already done in the exam)
Upper Extremity Complaint: Screens for Symmetry, Palpates for TART, AND Screens for Joint Motions Flex/Ext/AD/AB/IR/ER BL 15
