Musculoskeletal System Exams for the Knee
*P0 Test / Maneuver
Gait Analysis: Ask the patient to walk normally AND include one other: Rise from chair without using arms, OR Tandem/heel to toe 1
OSE Static — Anterior View: Facial Symmetry, Head/Neck Head Carriage, Shoulder Angle Hts, Thoracic Cage Position, Pelvis-Iliac Crest Hts, Hip-Greater Trochanter Hts, Knee-Tibia/Femur Angle or Condyle Hts, Ankle-Medial Arch or Malleoli Hts BL 2
OSE Static — Lateral View: Cervical, Thoracic, and Lumbar Curves, Head-External Auditory Meatus, Humerus-Greater Tuberosity, Femur-Greater Trochanter, Femur-Lateral Condyle, Ankle-Lateral Malleolus Alignments BL 3
OSE Static — Posterior View: Head-Mastoid Process Hts, Shoulder Angle Hts, Inferior Scapular Angle Hts, Spine Curves, Pelvis-Iliac Crest Hts, Hip-Greater Trochanter Hts, Knee-Tibia/Femur Angle or Condyle Hts, Ankle-Lateral Malleoli Hts BL 4
Inspect Thoracic, Lumbar, and Hip (usually affected joint, joint above, joint below, and adjacent joints): Inspect the skin for abrasions, bruising, scars, muscle for bulk and tone, and symmetry of the affected region BL 5
Palpate Thoracic, Lumbar, and Hip: Palpate for TART BL 6
AROM of the Thoracic Spine: Flex, Ext, Sidebend R, Sidebend L, Rotate R, Rotate L 7
AROM of the Lumbar Spine: Flex, Ext, Sidebend R, Sidebend L, Rotate R, Rotate L 8
AROM of the Hips: Flex, Ext, AB, AD, IR, ER BL 9
PROM of the Lumbar Spine: Flex, Ext, Sidebend R, Sidebend L, Rotate R, Rotate L 10
Test Strength of the Lumbar Spine: Flex, Ext, Sidebend R, Sidebend L, Rotate R, Rotate L 11
Partial Peripheral Nervous System Exams (please ask permission if removing garments in sensitive areas)
Evaluate Any LE Myotomes not Tested Previously with Strength Testing: L2 hip flexion, L3 knee extension, L4 foot dorsiflexion, L5 big toe extension AND S1 foot eversion OR foot plantarflexion BL 12
LE Dermatomes: Use Light Touch to evaluate L1 anterior inguinal area, L2 anterior upper middle thigh, L3 medial knee, L4 medial malleolus, L5 interspace between toes 1 and 2, S1 lateral malleolus BL (ASIA sensory points are underlined) 13
LE Deep Tendon Reflexes: L4 Patellar Tendon AND S1 Achilles Tendon reflexes BL 14
OSE TART and Boney Regional Testing (which might have been already done in the exam)
Lower Extremity Complaint: Screens for Symmetry, Palpates for TART, AND Screens for Joint Motions Flex/Ext/AD/AB/IR/ER BL 15
